Heads up: Chuseok overlaps the Seoul days

Chuseok 2026 runs Thursday Sept 24 through Saturday Sept 26 — that's Days 5, 6, and 7 of this trip. Day 5 and the Nami Island day trip (Day 7) are well-suited to the holiday since palaces and nature spots are common Chuseok activities. Day 6 (Fri 25) is the actual holiday day and carries the most risk of closures — flagged throughout that day's plan with backup options.

Seoul

Chuseok day itself — tower, shopping, Seongsu & Gangnam

⚑ This is the actual Chuseok holiday (the full moon day) — highest chance of closures

Today's the one day worth building in real flexibility for. Smaller independent restaurants and cafes — including some in Seongsu, which is full of small owner-run spots — are the most likely to close so owners can be with family. Major chains and malls (Olive Young's flagship, Sulbing, COEX/Starfield) almost always stay open since they cater heavily to tourists — those should be reliable. One genuine upside: Seoul itself gets noticeably quieter today as locals leave the city, so sightseeing and subways should be easier than a normal day.

  1. 09:30

    Breakfast

  2. 10:30

    N Seoul Tower

    Cable car up from near Myeongdong station — skip the hike up.

Gapyeong (day trip)

Nami Island & Garden of Morning Calm

⚑ Last day of Chuseok — book the shuttle tour ahead, build in traffic buffer

Nature-based day trips like this are actually well-suited to Chuseok — Nami Island and the Garden of Morning Calm don't depend on shops or restaurants being open. Since this is the tail end of the holiday, the heaviest nationwide travel congestion (which peaks around the holiday itself) should be easing, but highway traffic out to Gapyeong may still run heavier than a normal Saturday — worth padding the schedule a little and booking the shuttle earlier than usual, since domestic travelers often combine the tail of Chuseok with day trips too.
