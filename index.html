Heads up: Chuseok overlaps the Seoul days

Chuseok 2026 runs Thursday Sept 24 through Saturday Sept 26 — that's Days 5, 6, and 7 of this trip. Day 5 and the Nami Island day trip (Day 7) are well-suited to the holiday since palaces and nature spots are common Chuseok activities. Day 6 (Fri 25) is the actual holiday day and carries the most risk of closures — flagged throughout that day's plan with backup options.

Busan, downtown side — Gamcheon, Daegaksa & BIFF

Gamcheon in the morning, then down into Nampo-dong for a hidden temple, the tower, and BIFF Square saved for the end — its street food stalls pick up more in the late afternoon and evening, so it doubles as dinner.

  1. 08:00–08:30

    Gamcheon's food scene doesn't really get going until later in the morning, so breakfast is better handled before heading out.

  2. 08:30–09:00

    Taxi to Gamcheon Culture Village

  3. 09:00–11:30

    Gamcheon Culture Village

    Colorful hillside village, murals, photo spots.

    Some stairs and slopes — pace it, plenty of benches
  4. 11:30–13:00

    Lunch — Bibibim (비비빔)

    South Korea, Busan, Saha-gu, 감천제2동 10-3 1F — right by the village entrance. Korean classics without the soup: kimchi dumplings, bulgogi, japchae, savory pancakes. Vegetarian/vegan options available too.

  5. 13:00–13:40

    To Daegaksa Temple

    Bus down to Toseong Station (~10–15 min) → Line 1 subway to Nampo Station (~3 min) → walk via Gwangbok-ro to Gwangbokjungang-ro (~5–10 min). The temple's small entrance gate is easy to miss — it's right on the edge of Gukje Market.

  6. 13:40–14:10

    Daegaksa Temple

    6 Sinchang-dong 1(il)-ga, Jung-gu, Busan. A small, easy-to-miss temple tucked into the busy Nampo-dong streets — Japanese colonial-era architecture, tatami floors, a quiet contrast to the shopping outside.

  7. 14:10–16:30

    Busan Tower (Yongdusan Park)

    City views, easy walk up through the park.

    Cafe stop here if anyone wants to sit down
  8. 16:30–18:00

    BIFF Square — dinner at No.18 Wandang House (18번완당집)

    Famous for wontons with wrappers so thin and silky they're nicknamed "swallow wings" — served in a clear, smoky anchovy and seaweed broth.

    What to order: the Wandang+Momi combo (wonton soup with cold buckwheat noodles), or a bowl of wonton soup paired with their crispy pan-fried gyoza dumplings.

    Try Ssiat Hotteok (씨앗호떡) — a seed-filled version of the classic Korean pancake, a BIFF Square specialty — while wandering the square beforehand
  9. 18:00

    Back to Airbnb — rest, pack for Seoul

    Taxi or subway from Nampo-dong to Millak-ro, ~20–25 min. Optional if energy allows: Samgwangsa Temple, known for its lanterns — worth noting it's a real extra trip (~30 min from downtown), not a quick add-on.

Seoul

Chuseok day itself — Seongsu, Ttukseom, COEX, the Tower & Myeongdong

⚑ This is the actual Chuseok holiday (the full moon day) — highest chance of closures

Seongsu and the Han River picnic don't depend on shops being open, and COEX/Starfield reliably stays open through the holiday since it caters to tourists — so today should hold up well despite the closures elsewhere. With taxi/Uber instead of subway, the route doesn't need to follow train lines — it's a loop: Seongsu → Ttukseom → Gangnam → Tower (shorter visit) → Myeongdong for dinner, close to the Airbnb.

  1. 08:30–09:00

    Breakfast — Taegukdang

    7 Dongho-ro 24-gil — literally the same street as the Airbnb. Seoul's oldest bakery (since 1946), known for Monaca ice cream sandwiches and Sarada (salad) bread, with a striking old-fashioned interior. Opens 8:30am.

  2. 09:15

    Taxi/Uber to Seongsu

    From the Airbnb, ~20–25 min depending on traffic.

  3. 09:45–11:30

    Seongsu-dong

    Converted-warehouse district — design studios, industrial-chic cafes, boutique shopping.

    Chuseok caveat: this area leans heavily on small independent businesses, so more spots than usual may be closed today. Worth having a couple of backup cafes in mind rather than one fixed plan.
  4. 11:30–12:30

    Lunch — Le Freak

    A well-known Nashville-style hot chicken burger spot — one of the first places in Seongsu to go viral, with a simple 9-item menu. A proper sit-down meal rather than another cafe stop.

    Lunch last order 14:30, but the place gets busy — arriving right at 11:30 opening helps avoid a wait
  5. 12:30–12:45

    Taxi/Uber to Ttukseom Han River Park

    ~10–15 min — much shorter than the subway route, since it's a direct drive rather than a line transfer.

  6. 12:45–14:15

    Picnic — Ttukseom Han River Park

    Head toward the riverside grass (Music Fountain Square / Plaza Area). For a good sunset spot, pick grass closer to the water so Jamsil Bridge is visible without trees blocking the view.

  7. 14:15

    Han River ramyeon

    A GS25 or CU convenience store inside the park sells instant ramyeon in paper bowls, plus an automatic hot-water machine right outside to cook it yourself — a genuine local pastime, not just a snack stop.

    Optional: BHC Chicken nearby — order Sunsal (boneless), Sunsal Bburinkle (crispy chicken with sweet-savory cheese powder), or Matcho-King (soy-garlic-chili chicken). Takes ~15 min to prepare — grab it and drinks from a nearby minimart, then walk to the grass.
  8. 14:45–15:05

    Taxi/Uber to Gangnam / COEX

    ~15–20 min, crossing the river.

  9. 15:05–16:30

    Gangnam — T1 Building & COEX

    The Gangnam Style statue is right outside COEX. Starfield Library inside COEX — malls like this reliably stay open through Chuseok.

  10. 16:30–17:00

    Taxi/Uber to N Seoul Tower

    ~20–25 min — heading back toward the Airbnb's side of the city.

  11. 17:00–18:15

    N Seoul Tower

    Sunset in late September is around 6:30–7pm, so this window still catches both daylight views and the city lighting up.

  12. 18:15–18:45

    To Myeongdong

    Close by — a short taxi or walk down from Namsan, ~15–20 min.

  13. 18:45–20:15

    Myeongdong — dinner & a casual wander

    Plenty of reliable dinner options, with some easy browsing since you're there anyway.

  14. 20:15

    Back to the Airbnb — pack

    Get bags sorted before bed — early pickup tomorrow for Gapyeong.
